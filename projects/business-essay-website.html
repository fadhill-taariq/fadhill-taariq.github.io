Business Essay Website

A Django-powered multi-page site that presents an academic essay on American business history as a small, calm web publication — closer to a long-form article than a coursework submission.

The brief

A coursework essay deserves more than a single PDF. I wanted to publish my Business History essay the way I'd want to read it: broken into chapters, paced for screens, easy to navigate, and quietly typeset. Django gave me the structure to do that without overcomplicating things.

The build

Django handles routing between essay sections; templates provide a shared layout so every page feels like the same publication; static files keep the typography and spacing consistent. The result is a small site where the essay reads naturally on any screen and the structure of the argument is visible from the menu.
